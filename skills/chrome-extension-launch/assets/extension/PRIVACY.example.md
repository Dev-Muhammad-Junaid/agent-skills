# Privacy Policy for Distract

**Last Updated:** September 30, 2026

Distract ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Distract Chrome Extension.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

The extension is designed to operate locally on your device.

- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identification information (PII) to external servers.
- **Local Storage:** The list of page sections you chose to hide, and whether Distract is paused on each site, are stored with Chrome's `storage.sync` API so your choices persist across your own signed-in Chrome browsers. Nothing else is stored.
- **Supported Sites:** The extension runs only on YouTube, X (Twitter), Facebook and Instagram. There, it reads the structure of the page in your browser only to find and hide the sections you picked, and to draw the page map in its popup. It does not read, store, or send your posts, messages, feeds, account details, or browsing history.

## 2. Third-Party Services

The extension does not use any third-party analytics or tracking services, does not show ads, loads no remote code, and makes no network requests of its own.

## 3. Data Security

All data remains within your local browser environment. We never have access to your accounts, passwords, or authentication tokens. Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored.

## 4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
